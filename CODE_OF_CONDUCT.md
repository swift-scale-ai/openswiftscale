# Community code of conduct

OpenSwiftScale is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Discuss ideas and technical evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while protecting project safety.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's credentials, prompts, customer data, or identifying information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and provider affiliations when they are relevant to a proposal.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused, reproducible, and accessible to future contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, credential disclosure, sustained disruption, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not accepted in project-controlled spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected participants.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@swift-scale.com`. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports made in good faith will be reviewed without retaliation.

This code applies to repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other spaces where someone represents the OpenSwiftScale project.
